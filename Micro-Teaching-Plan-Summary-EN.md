# Micro-Teaching Plan Summary: Transdisciplinary and Critical Thinking

**Course**: PDEV 6800X - Introduction to Teaching and Learning in Higher Education  
**Team Size**: 4 members  
**Duration**: 20 minutes + 5 minutes Q&A  
**Topic**: Transdisciplinary and Critical Thinking

---

## Executive Summary

This comprehensive micro-teaching plan provides a complete guide for delivering a 20-minute interactive tutorial on **"Developing Transdisciplinary Thinking: From Problem Identification to Innovative Solutions"**. The plan includes detailed topic rationale, division of labor for 4 team members, and a slide-by-slide PPT outline with 12 slides.

---

## Part 1: Topic Selection

### Recommended Topic: "Developing Transdisciplinary Thinking: From Problem Identification to Innovative Solutions"

### Why This Topic?

1. **Important & Practical Knowledge** ✅
   - Transdisciplinary thinking is one of the most critical transferable skills in 21st century education and workplace
   - Covers all Session ILOs requirements (Sessions 1-4)

2. **Fits 20-Minute Format** ⏱️
   - Clear 4-module structure (5 minutes each):
     - Introduction & Concepts
     - Core Principles Framework
     - Interactive Case Analysis
     - Summary & Self-Assessment

3. **Highly Interactive** 🎯
   - Real-world case studies (Climate change, AI ethics, Public health)
   - Active learning strategies (Think-Pair-Share, group discussions)
   - Digital tools integration (Kahoot, Miro/Padlet, Google Forms)

4. **Perfect for 4-Member Team** 👥
   - Student A: Host & Opening/Closing
   - Student B: Theory Explanation
   - Student C: Case Analysis Facilitator
   - Student D: Digital Tools Manager & Visual Designer

---

## Part 2: Division of Labor

### Student A: Host & Lead Instructor 1
**Overall Coordinator + Opening & Closing**

**Preparation (1-2 weeks before)**:
- Coordinate team meetings and task timeline
- Collect real transdisciplinary cases
- Design Kahoot quiz questions (5-7 questions)
- Prepare PPT Slides 1-3

**Delivery (20-minute class)**:
- **0-3 min**: Opening, introduce learning objectives, lead Kahoot quiz
- **18-20 min**: Summary and self-assessment rubric

**Q&A (5 minutes)**:
- Facilitate Q&A session, distribute questions to team members

---

### Student B: Theory Instructor (Lead Instructor 2)
**Core Content - Transdisciplinary Thinking Framework**

**Preparation**:
- Research transdisciplinary thinking theories
- Extract 4 core principles (Integration, Multiple Perspectives, Synthesis, Innovation)
- Design framework diagrams (flowcharts/mind maps)
- Prepare PPT Slides 4-6

**Delivery**:
- **3-8 min**: Explain core principles with comparison table (Disciplinary vs Transdisciplinary)
- Support Student C during interactive activities

---

### Student C: Case Analysis Facilitator & Active Learning Designer
**Interactive Activity Designer & Facilitator**

**Preparation**:
- Design interactive case analysis activity
- Prepare case background (e.g., AI in medical diagnosis ethics)
- Design Think-Pair-Share activity flow
- Prepare PPT Slides 7-8
- Prepare facilitation question list

**Delivery**:
- **8-15 min**:
  - Case introduction (2 min)
  - Think-Pair-Share activity (5 min)
  - Class sharing & provide feedback (3 min)

---

### Student D: Digital Tools Expert & Visual Designer
**Technical Support + PPT Creation + Summary**

**Preparation**:
- Design complete PPT (12 slides) with consistent visual style
- Set up Kahoot quiz (5-7 questions)
- Prepare Miro/Padlet collaboration board
- Create self-assessment rubric (Google Form)
- Prepare PPT Slides 9-12

**Delivery**:
- **0-3 min**: Operate Kahoot quiz
- **8-15 min**: Operate Miro/Padlet, capture student ideas
- **15-20 min**: Explain self-assessment rubric, summary

## Part 3: PPT Slide-by-Slide Outline

**Total Slides**: 12  
**Design Style**: Clean, professional, visual  
**Color Scheme**: HKUST(GZ) official red (#BB0E3D) + neutral colors  

---

### Slide 1: Title Slide
**Speaker**: Student A (0-0.5 min)

**Content**:
- Title: "Developing Transdisciplinary Thinking: From Problem Identification to Innovative Solutions"
- Subtitle: Transdisciplinary and Critical Thinking
- Team members: Students A, B, C, D
- Course info: PDEV 6800X Micro-Teaching | Fall 2025

**Design Tip**: Use high-quality background image (puzzle, network connections) symbolizing "integration"

**Guide Alignment**: Session 3 ILO-1 (Professional presentation), Quality of Materials (20%)

---

### Slide 2: Opening Engagement - Kahoot Quiz
**Speaker**: Student A explains + Student D operates (0.5-3 min)

**Content**:
- Title: "Let's Start with a Quick Quiz! 🎯"
- Display QR code for Kahoot quiz
- 5 quiz questions testing initial understanding

**Design Tip**: Large, centered QR code with animation effects

**Guide Alignment**: Session 1 ILO-3 (Active learning), Session 4 ILO-2 (Digital tools), Audience Engagement (25%)

---

### Slide 3: Learning Objectives
**Speaker**: Student A (3-4 min)

**Content**:
- Title: "What Will You Learn Today?"
- 4 learning objectives:
  1. 🎯 Understand transdisciplinary thinking definition and importance
  2. 🧠 Master four core principles
  3. 🔍 Apply to real-world case analysis
  4. ✅ Assess your own transdisciplinary thinking ability

**Design Tip**: Numbered icon list with color-coded cognitive levels

**Guide Alignment**: Session 1 ILO-1 (Outcome-based teaching), Clarity of Content (25%)

---

### Slide 4: What is Transdisciplinary Thinking?
**Speaker**: Student B (4-5 min)

**Content**:
- Definition: "Integrating knowledge, methods, and perspectives from multiple disciplines to address complex real-world problems"
- Core characteristics:
  - Transcends disciplinary boundaries
  - Focuses on real-world problems
  - Emphasizes synthesis & innovation
- Visual: Venn diagram showing disciplinary intersections

**Design Tip**: Multi-colored Venn diagram with "Transdisciplinary Solution" in center

**Guide Alignment**: Session 1 ILO-1 (Outcome-based theories), Session 3 ILO-1 (Visual tools)

---

### Slide 5: Why Transdisciplinary Thinking?
**Speaker**: Student B (5-6 min)

**Content**:
- Three reasons:
  1. Complex problems cannot be solved by single disciplines
  2. 21st century core workplace competency (WEF Future of Jobs Report)
  3. Source of innovation (breakthroughs at disciplinary intersections)

**Design Tip**: 3-column layout with icons and brief examples

**Guide Alignment**: Session 1 ILO-1 (Align with learning outcomes), Clarity of Content (25%)

---

### Slide 6: Four Core Principles
**Speaker**: Student B (6-8 min)

**Content**:
- Four principles (flowchart/mind map):
  1. **Integration** - Integrate theories, methods, data from different disciplines
  2. **Multiple Perspectives** - Examine from technical, social, economic, ethical angles
  3. **Synthesis & Critical Evaluation** - Identify bias, evaluate evidence, synthesize conclusions
  4. **Innovation** - Propose novel, cross-boundary solutions

**Design Tip**: Sequential boxes with arrows, color-coded with icons

**Guide Alignment**: Session 1 ILO-1 (Knowledge framework), Session 3 ILO-1 (Visual tools), Quality of Materials (20%)

---

### Slide 7: Comparison Table - Disciplinary vs Transdisciplinary
**Speaker**: Student B (8 min) → Transition to Student C

**Content**:
- Comparison table:

| Dimension | Disciplinary | Transdisciplinary |
|-----------|--------------|-------------------|
| Focus | Discipline-internal problems | Real-world complex problems |
| Knowledge Source | Single discipline | Multi-disciplinary integration |
| Perspective | Single viewpoint | Multiple dimensions |
| Solution | Discipline-specific answer | Comprehensive innovative solution |
| Example | AI from CS perspective only | AI integrating tech, ethics, law, sociology |

**Design Tip**: Clear table with contrasting colors

**Guide Alignment**: Session 3 ILO-1 (Clear presentation), Clarity of Content (25%)

---

### Slide 8: Case Study Introduction - AI in Medical Diagnosis
**Speaker**: Student C (8-9 min)

**Content**:
- Title: "Case Study: AI in Medical Diagnosis"
- Scenario: Hospital uses AI for skin cancer diagnosis
- Technical advantage: 95% accuracy
- Challenges:
  - Data bias (70% accuracy for dark skin patients)
  - Patient privacy
  - Liability issues
  - Social impact
- Question: "How to solve this using transdisciplinary thinking?"

**Design Tip**: Medical images with highlighted problem areas (red boxes)

**Guide Alignment**: Session 1 ILO-3 (Active learning case), Session 3 ILO-2 (Open-ended questions)

---

### Slide 9: Interactive Activity - Think-Pair-Share
**Speaker**: Student C facilitates + Student D operates Miro/Padlet (9-15 min)

**Content**:
- Title: "Group Activity: Think-Pair-Share 🤝"
- Activity guide:
  1. **Think** (1 min): Individual reflection
  2. **Pair/Small Group** (3 min): Group discussion (2-4 people)
  3. **Share** (3 min): Class sharing + submit ideas on Miro/Padlet
- Display Miro/Padlet QR code

**Design Tip**: Three-step flowchart with time indicators and icons

**Guide Alignment**: Session 1 ILO-3 (Active learning), Session 4 ILO-2 (Digital tools), Audience Engagement (25%), Facilitation (25%)

---

### Slide 10: Case Analysis Summary - Student Ideas
**Speaker**: Student C + Student D (15-16 min)

**Content**:
- Title: "What Did We Discover? 💡"
- Summary of student ideas (from Miro/Padlet):
  - Disciplines involved: CS, Medicine, Ethics, Law, Sociology
  - Transdisciplinary solutions:
    1. Tech + Ethics: Diverse training dataset
    2. Law + Medicine: Clear liability framework
    3. Sociology + Medicine: Doctor-AI collaboration model
- Instructor feedback with specific praise and suggestions

**Design Tip**: Screenshots with color-coded discipline labels

**Guide Alignment**: Session 2 ILO-2 (Meaningful feedback), Session 3 ILO-2 (Facilitation), Facilitation of Interactions (25%)

---

### Slide 11: Self-Assessment Rubric
**Speaker**: Student D (16-18 min)

**Content**:
- Title: "Self-Assessment: How Transdisciplinary Are You? 📊"
- Formative assessment tool
- 4 dimensions (1-5 scale each):
  1. Integration ability
  2. Multiple perspectives
  3. Critical evaluation
  4. Innovation
- Display Google Form QR code

**Design Tip**: Rubric table with QR code and progress bar icons

**Guide Alignment**: Session 2 ILO-2 (Feedback tool design), Session 1 ILO-1 (Formative assessment), Session 4 ILO-1&2 (Digital tools)

---

### Slide 12: Summary & Further Learning
**Speaker**: Student A + Student D (18-20 min)

**Content**:
- Title: "Key Takeaways & Further Learning 🚀"
- Key points review (echo Slide 3 objectives):
  ✅ Transdisciplinary thinking = Integrate multi-disciplinary knowledge  
  ✅ Four core principles: Integration, Multiple Perspectives, Synthesis, Innovation  
  ✅ Real case practice: AI medical diagnosis ethics  
  ✅ Self-assessment completed
- Further learning resources:
  - 📚 Books: "The Third Culture", "Range"
  - 🌐 Online courses: Coursera "Critical Thinking & Problem Solving"
  - 🔗 Canvas Discussion Board
- "Any questions?" 🙋‍♀️

**Design Tip**: Checklist style with resource icons

**Guide Alignment**: Session 1 ILO-1 (Echo learning objectives), Session 3 ILO-1 (Clear closing), Quality of Materials (20%)

## Success Factors

### Rehearsal & Preparation Checklist
- [ ] At least 2 full rehearsals ensuring 20-minute timing
- [ ] Test all digital tools with Plan B ready
- [ ] Prepare Q&A anticipated questions and answers
- [ ] Clear transition cues between team members

### Delivery Best Practices
- [ ] Arrive 15 minutes early to test equipment
- [ ] Maintain eye contact and audience engagement
- [ ] Use positive body language
- [ ] Repeat questions before answering
- [ ] Time check at end of each section

### Peer Grading Tips
- [ ] Provide specific, constructive, relevant feedback
- [ ] Example ✅: "Your Kahoot quiz effectively checked understanding. Consider adding one more case-based question."
- [ ] Avoid ❌: "Good quiz."
