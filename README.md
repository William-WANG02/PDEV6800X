## 📊 Plan Highlights

### Topic: "Developing Transdisciplinary Thinking: From Problem Identification to Innovative Solutions"

- **Duration**: 20 minutes + 5 minutes Q&A
- **Team Size**: 4 members
- **Slides**: 12 slides total
- **Digital Tools**: Kahoot, Miro/Padlet, Google Forms

### Key Features
✅ Fully aligned with Session 1-4 ILOs  
✅ Multiple active learning strategies (Kahoot quiz, Think-Pair-Share, case analysis)  
✅ 3 digital tools integration  
✅ Formative assessment with self-evaluation rubric  
✅ Clear 4-member division of labor  
✅ Real-world case study (AI in medical diagnosis ethics)  

## 🎓 Learning Objectives

By the end of this micro-teaching session, students will be able to:
1. 🎯 Understand transdisciplinary thinking definition and importance
2. 🧠 Master four core principles (Integration, Multiple Perspectives, Synthesis, Innovation)
3. 🔍 Apply transdisciplinary thinking to real-world case analysis
4. ✅ Assess their own transdisciplinary thinking abilities

## 📖 How to Use This Plan

### For Team Members (4 people):
1. Read the full plan document (Chinese or English version)
2. Review your assigned role:
   - **Student A**: Host & Lead Instructor 1 (Opening & Closing)
   - **Student B**: Lead Instructor 2 (Theory Framework)
   - **Student C**: Facilitator & Case Analysis Host
   - **Student D**: Digital Tools Expert & Visual Designer
3. Follow the preparation checklist in your section
4. Rehearse at least 2 times as a team
5. Test all digital tools before presentation
