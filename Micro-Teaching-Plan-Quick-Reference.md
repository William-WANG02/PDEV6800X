# PDEV6800X Micro-Teaching Plan - Quick Reference Guide

## Overview
- **Group Size**: 4 members
- **Duration**: 20 minutes teaching + 5 minutes Q&A
- **Topic**: Communication Skills - "Effective Presentation in Academic Settings"

## Topic Selection Rationale

**Why this topic?**
1. ✅ Directly aligned with Lecture 3 (Presentation & Facilitation Skills)
2. ✅ Integrates content from Lectures 1-3 seamlessly
3. ✅ Perfect fit for 20-minute delivery with multiple interaction points
4. ✅ Highly relevant to graduate student audience
5. ✅ Allows for clear 4-person division of labor
6. ✅ Meets all grading rubric requirements

## Team Roles

| Role | Member | Responsibilities | Time |
|------|--------|------------------|------|
| **Lead Instructor A** | Student A | Opening + PPT Design Principles | 0-8 min |
| **Lead Instructor B** | Student B | Delivery Techniques + Q&A Strategies | 8-16 min |
| **Interaction Facilitator** | Student C | Active Learning Activities + Feedback Collection | Throughout |
| **Technical Coordinator** | Student D | Digital Tools + Time Management | Throughout |

## Session Structure (20 minutes)

### Part 1: Introduction & Engagement (0-5 min)
- Opening and Learning Outcomes (Student A)
- Hook Activity: Think-Pair-Share (Student C)
- Mentimeter Word Cloud (Student D)

### Part 2: PPT Design Principles (5-9 min)
- 7×7 Rule, Fonts, Colors (Student A)
- Good vs Bad PPT Comparison (Student A)

### Part 3: Delivery Techniques (9-15 min)
- Verbal & Non-verbal Cues (Student B)
- 3MT Video Case Study (Student B + C)

### Part 4: Q&A Strategies (15-18 min)
- STAR Method (Student B)
- Handling Difficult Questions (Student B)

### Part 5: Conclusion & Assessment (18-20 min)
- Summary of Key Takeaways (Student A)
- Kahoot Quick Quiz (Student D)

### Q&A Session (20-25 min)
- Moderated by Student C
- All members participate in answering

## PPT Slides (20 slides)

1. **Title Slide** - Course title and team members
2. **Agenda** - Session roadmap
3. **Learning Outcomes** - 3 action-oriented objectives
4. **Hook Question** - Think-Pair-Share activity
5. **Mentimeter Results** - Word cloud display
6. **Transition: Part 1** - PPT Design section
7. **7×7 Rule** - Slide design principle
8. **Fonts & Colors** - Design guidelines
9. **Good vs Bad PPT** - Case comparison
10. **Transition: Part 2** - Delivery section
11. **Verbal Delivery** - Do's and Don'ts
12. **Non-Verbal Cues** - Body language tips
13. **3MT Case Study** - Video analysis activity
14. **Transition: Part 3** - Q&A section
15. **STAR Method** - Q&A strategy
16. **Difficult Questions** - Handling strategies
17. **Summary** - Key takeaways
18. **Kahoot Quiz** - Interactive assessment
19. **Q&A Session** - Open discussion
20. **Thank You** - Feedback QR code

## Active Learning Strategies

1. **Think-Pair-Share** (Slide 4) - Recalling memorable presentations
2. **Case Analysis** (Slide 13) - Analyzing 3MT video
3. **Interactive Quiz** (Slide 18) - Kahoot assessment

## Alignment with Grading Rubric

### Instructor Grading (60%)
- ✅ Clear Learning Objectives (OBTL)
- ✅ 3 Active Learning Strategies
- ✅ Meaningful Feedback mechanisms
- ✅ Effective Presentation Skills demonstration
- ✅ Facilitation Skills display
- ✅ Digital Tool integration

### Peer Grading (30%)
- ✅ **Clarity**: Structured content, clear flow
- ✅ **Engagement**: Multiple interactive activities
- ✅ **Digital Tools**: 3+ tools effectively used
- ✅ **Facilitation**: Well-organized interactions

### Feedback Quality (10%)
- ✅ Specific, constructive, relevant feedback solicited

## Resource Checklist

### Technical
- [ ] Projector & laptop tested
- [ ] Stable internet connection
- [ ] Mentimeter account & questions prepared
- [ ] Kahoot account & quiz created (3-5 questions)
- [ ] Google Form feedback survey ready
- [ ] 3MT video downloaded (backup)

### Materials
- [ ] Complete PPT (18-20 slides + PDF backup)
- [ ] Timer/stopwatch
- [ ] Time prompt cards for presenters
- [ ] Simple rubric for peer assessment

### Backup Plans
- [ ] Paper-based activities if internet fails
- [ ] PPT screenshots if video fails
- [ ] Identified sections to skip if time runs short

## Success Criteria

### Quantitative
- ✅ Complete within 20 minutes (±1 minute)
- ✅ At least 70% audience participation in activities
- ✅ Kahoot quiz average accuracy ≥70%
- ✅ Collect feedback from ≥80% of audience

### Qualitative
- ✅ Audience can recall 2+ key points
- ✅ Positive feedback on interactivity and practicality
- ✅ Smooth team collaboration
- ✅ Peer grading score ≥3.5/5

## Risk Management

| Risk | Prevention | Response |
|------|------------|----------|
| **Time overrun** | Strict timing, Student D monitors | Skip case discussion if needed |
| **Low participation** | Engaging hook, clear instructions | Call on individuals, simplify activities |
| **Technical failure** | Pre-test all tech, backup materials | Use paper version or verbal activities |
| **Q&A silence** | Prepare 2-3 seed questions | Facilitator prompts discussion |
| **Member absence** | Full rehearsal for all | Others cover, adjust roles |
