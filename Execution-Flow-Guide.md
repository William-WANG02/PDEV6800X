## 互动活动详细指引 / Activity Detailed Instructions

### Activity 1: Think-Pair-Share (3-5 min)
**Facilitator:** Student C

**Script:**
```
"Before we dive into the content, let's take a moment to reflect.
Think back to the most memorable presentation you've ever attended.
What made it stand out? [PAUSE 30 seconds]

Now, turn to the person next to you and share your experience.
You have 1 minute each. [PAUSE 2 minutes]

Let's hear from a few of you. Who would like to share?"
[Select 2-3 volunteers, 20-30 seconds each]

"Great! Notice the common themes: [summarize briefly].
These are exactly what we'll explore today."
```

---

### Activity 2: 3MT Case Analysis (12-15 min)
**Facilitator:** Student C (with Student B)

**Script:**
```
"Now we're going to watch a 1-minute clip from a 3 Minute Thesis competition.
As you watch, notice:
- What presentation techniques does the speaker use well?
- What could be improved?

[Student D plays video]

Now, in small groups of 2-3, discuss these two questions.
You have 2 minutes. [PAUSE 2 minutes]

Let's hear your observations. [Call on 2-3 groups]
Group 1, what did you notice?"
```

---

### Activity 3: Kahoot Quiz (18-20 min)
**Coordinator:** Student D

**Questions (prepare 3-5):**
1. What is the 7×7 rule for PPT slides?
   - a) 7 words per line, 7 lines per slide ✓
   - b) 7 slides per presentation
   - c) 7 colors per slide
   - d) 7 fonts per presentation

2. True or False: You should read directly from your slides when presenting.
   - a) True
   - b) False ✓

3. What does the "S" in STAR method stand for?
   - a) Start
   - b) Situation ✓
   - c) Stop
   - d) Summary

4. Which is a good non-verbal delivery technique?
   - a) Reading from notes
   - b) Maintaining eye contact ✓
   - c) Facing the screen
   - d) Staying completely still

5. What should you do if you don't know the answer to a question?
   - a) Make up an answer
   - b) Ignore the question
   - c) Admit it and offer to follow up ✓
   - d) Blame the question

**Script:**
```
"Let's test what we've learned today with a quick quiz!
Go to kahoot.it and enter the PIN: [display PIN]
[Wait for everyone to join]
Let's begin!"
[Run through questions, celebrate correct answers]
"Great job! Our winner is... [announce top 3]"
```

## 应急预案快速参考 / Emergency Quick Reference

### If Running OVER TIME:
**At 17 min and still in Part 2:**
- Skip Slide 16 (difficult questions example)
- Go straight to Summary (Slide 17)
- Shorten Kahoot to 3 questions only

### If Running UNDER TIME:
**At 16 min and already at Summary:**
- Add a bonus question in Kahoot
- Extend Q&A discussion
- Ask audience: "Any questions about what we covered?"

### If Tech Fails:
**Mentimeter/Kahoot down:**
- Use paper notes: "Write your answer and hold it up"
- Verbal responses: "Show of hands"

**Video won't play:**
- Use Slide 13 to describe the 3MT example verbally
- Ask audience: "Has anyone seen a 3MT presentation? What stood out?"

### If Audience Not Engaging:
- **Call on individuals**: "What do you think, [name]?"
- **Make it personal**: "Think of your own research presentation..."
- **Gamify**: "First person to answer gets a bonus point!"

## 成功的最后建议 / Final Tips for Success

### 🎯 For Speakers (Students A & B):
- **Smile** and make **eye contact** with the audience
- **Pause** after key points to let them sink in
- **Use gestures** to emphasize important concepts
- If you lose your place, **breathe and check your notes** - it's okay!
- **Enjoy it!** Your enthusiasm is contagious

### 🎯 For Facilitator (Student C):
- **Listen actively** to audience responses
- **Appreciate all contributions**: "Great point!", "Thank you for sharing!"
- **Be flexible** - if an activity runs short, don't rush; if it runs long, politely wrap up
- **Watch the room** - if people look confused, ask presenters to clarify

### 🎯 For Tech Coordinator (Student D):
- **Stay calm** if tech issues arise - you've got backup plans!
- **Be discreet** with time signals - don't distract the audience
- **Anticipate** the next slide - be ready to advance smoothly
- **Test, test, test** - the best tech support is prevention

### 🎯 For Everyone:
- **Trust your teammates** - you've prepared well together
- **Support each other** - if someone stumbles, help smoothly
- **Have fun** - this is a learning experience for everyone
- **Remember**: The audience wants you to succeed!
